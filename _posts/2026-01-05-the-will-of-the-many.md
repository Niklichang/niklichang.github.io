"They ask something small of you. A thing you would prefer not to do, but is not so terrible. You think you are working your way up, but in fact they are changing you. Moulding you into what they think you should be, one compromise at a time. I am just saying that in this place... each man has to find his line."

<br/>

There is a unique feeling of getting lost in the realms of a book, distinctly different from movies, sports, or any other endeavor. We emerge, maybe not enlightened, but a bit more wise than we were before.

8/10.
